Open your SamplePad's card

PADDOCK reads the kits and sounds on the card. Nothing gets written until you press Save to card, and every kit it replaces is backed up first.

  1. On the pad: hold ▲ + ◀ for 2 seconds, pick Load Samples/Kits Via USB, press ▶. The card shows up as a drive on your computer. (Or put the SD card in a card reader. That's much faster. Or open a backup copy of the card to look around.)
  2. Click the button and pick that drive (or folder).

This browser can't open folders. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.

When you're done: on the pad, hold ◀ + ▲ for 2 seconds to leave USB mode.

Make the pad start on this kit

The pad always powers on into kit 1, and its own kits 1–10 can't be saved over (SAVE KIT and COPY KIT won't go there). The kits on the card come after them, from kit 11. So after power-on, highlight the kit number and tap ▶ up to this kit (it): ten quick taps reach kit 11.

Also worth doing once, so it never switches itself off mid-set:

  1. Hold ▲ + ◀ → Other → Auto-Shutdown → Off.
  2. Hold ▼ + ▶ (the Save menu) → SAVE GLOBAL SETTINGS → ▶.
  3. Highlight YES, press ▶. (Without the YES it's forgotten at power-off.)
